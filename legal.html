Personal information is stored on infrastructure located in Canada. To operate the Service, certain processing is performed on our behalf by service providers, comprising: a cloud database and authentication provider (Canada); an AI language-processing provider (United States); a voice-synthesis provider (United States), engaged only where you use voice features; and a transactional email provider. We process personal information to provide and secure the Service, to maintain conversational continuity, to evaluate Service performance, and to comply with legal obligations. Conversation content and personalization records are retained for the duration of your account. Upon deletion, data is removed from production systems immediately and from encrypted backups within 35 days through routine backup rotation. Waitlist email addresses are retained no longer than one year following the launch notification. Service metadata is retained thereafter only in aggregate, non-identifying form.

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it rectified, to have it deleted, to receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format, and to withdraw consent to its processing. Residents of Québec may lodge a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec; residents of the European Economic Area may lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority; residents of other jurisdictions may contact their local data protection authority.

8.Safeguards and Incident Notification

We employ technical and organizational safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, including access controls that restrict conversation data to the account that created it, server-side isolation of all credentials, and data minimization. In the event of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious injury, we will notify affected persons and the authorities required by law without undue delay and will maintain the incident register required by applicable legislation.
